F.A.Q.

Will I get banned from GTA Online for playing GTAMP?
No. GTAMP does not interact with Rockstar Games Online Services other than to validate your game copy the first time you launch it, and it doesn't modify your game files.
Is GTAMP the same as FiveM?
No. GTAMP is inspired by FiveM's architecture but is an entirely separate, own codebase.
Can I play GTAMP on my console?
Unfortunately not — GTAMP relies on interoperability mechanisms only available on the PC platform.
What does the MP stand for?
Multiplayer — GTAMP provides a FiveM-style multiplayer experience for GTA V.
My GTAMP crashes, what should I do?
Post a topic on the forum with the problems you are experiencing, including as much detail as you can.
I want to run a server, how can I do so?
Running your own server is easy. Refer to our server page to get started. Running into trouble? Our community is there to help on the forum.
