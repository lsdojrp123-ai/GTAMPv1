F.A.Q.

# Will I get banned from GTA Online for playing GTAMP?

No. GTAMP does not interact with Rockstar Games Online Services other than to validate your game copy the first time you launch it, and it doesn't modify your game files, so you don't have to do anything to switch between GTAMP and GTA Online.

# Is GTAMP the same as FiveM?

No. GTAMP is inspired by the same architecture ideas but is an entirely separate codebase of our own.

# Can I play GTAMP on my console?

Unfortunately not — GTAMP relies on mechanisms only available on the PC platform.

# My GTAMP crashes, what should I do?

Post a topic on the forum describing the problems you are experiencing, with as much detail as you can, so we can help you better.

# I want to run a server, how can I do so?

Running your own server is easy. Refer to our server page to get started. Running into trouble? Our community is there to help on the forum.
